**The one arm that reads as vestigial but is not**: the template-expansion bound
(`:956-959`). The composed bound (`:1171`) does **not** subsume its detection —
`appendReportSuffix` (`:181-188`) `trimEnd()`s the lane text and, for a terminal
envelope marker, rebuilds it as `body\n\nsuffix\n\n<marker>`, stripping the whitespace
around the marker, so the composed task can be **smaller** than the expansion (probe:
expansion 65 437 B → emitted task 60 108 B, exit 0). When an expansion's overage sits in
such a whitespace run, `:956-959` is the **only** detector (probe: a 65 610-byte expansion
is refused while that lane's composed text is ≈60 281 B, under the `:1171` bound) — so
removing it would *admit* the wave, not merely reword the refusal. It also keeps its unique
old-precedence diagnostic: `items[0] expansion exceeds 65536 bytes` fires ahead of the band
gates and `duplicate_expansion` (the same shape with a short item refuses `too_few_lanes`
instead), versus `items[0] prompt exceeds 65536 bytes after reportDir append and godspeed
invocation` at `:1171`. Its own retention statement is `:834-835` ("The expansion is still
validated exactly as before (64 KiB bound, pairwise-distinct expansions); hoist rewrites
EMISSION only"); `:1085-1090`/`:1190-1191` state the old-precedence rule for the **legacy
composed emission**, not for `:956-959`. Its message text is pinned by no test.

Deliberate byte-stability carve-outs (do not "simplify" them): legacy per-lane emission
composed first at old precedence; the all-or-nothing compaction with recorded reasons;
`spec.hoist: false`; LEGACY templates (leading invocation / terminal marker);
`MIN_HOIST_SAVINGS` 512; the legacy-registry carve-out that keeps the TOCTOU pin's
precedence; `planToDispatchPayload`'s two independent arms; the duplicate `--emit-tasks`
refusal at its old precedence; the floor never clamped to the cap (else `impossible_band`
is unreachable); `usedNames` stays inline next to `rejectDuplicate` for shape, not bytes
("a different shape (no carried tag) and stays inline", `:340-341`).

Deletion risks the source itself names: widening `RESUME_ID_PATTERN` re-opens the
unbounded-id hole; hoisting a leading invocation duplicates across context+task and a
terminal marker strands nonterminal in the context; removing the legacy path breaks
operators who opted out for a per-lane-fitting template that breaches the shared context
bound; arming the collision guard for legacy registries preempts the TOCTOU pin;
clamping the floor to the cap makes `impossible_band` unreachable; collapsing the payload's
two arms to `else-if` drops a both-field entry from `resumeSends`; re-splitting the
registry-doc rebuild re-opens the drifted `reportDir` guard.
