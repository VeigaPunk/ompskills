Source: r1 lane `SpecSchema` (fleet `shokunin3`, mission `receipt-keeper`, wave `receipt-keeper-r1-w1`); anchor launcher `sighting/tower-fleet.mjs` @ `ada7a90` (sha256 `80f0814e…`).

## Asked

What spec.json holds, its defaults, and which fleets have one.

## Observed

- `loadSpec` :319-415 requires `fleet`, `repo`, `ufoRoot`, non-empty `missions`; defaults `base` = repo HEAD branch, `roundCeiling` 1 (:388), `maxTime` "3h" (:390), `stateDir` `<repo>/.tower/fleets/<fleet>`, `worktreeRoot` `<repo>/.tower/worktrees/<fleet>`.
- The launcher never writes spec.json into the state dir; its presence there is operator convention.
- Census: shokunin 8 missions, shokunin2 8, tower1 16, towerprobe 1, shokunin3 10; all set `roundCeiling`/`maxTime` explicitly (2/4h, 2/4h, 2/3h, 1/1h, 2/4h).
- `tower1-safety-r2` has **no** spec.json: its fleet.json says `fleet: "tower1"` with `stateDir` overridden to `.tower/fleets/tower1-safety-r2`; the override spec is not on disk. Feeding its fleet.json back as `--spec` → `INVALID_SPEC`.
